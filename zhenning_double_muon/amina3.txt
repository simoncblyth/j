While the optical speedup on cluster will be substantially less that on my
GPU workstation (as no RT cores, so slower ray tracing) the overall speedup should 
still be high as in large part the overall speedup is limited by how much of the 
processing cannot be parallelized. See https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amdahl%27s_law

For muon simulation the parallelizable fraction is very high, perhaps 0.995 
or more : which is why Opticks can get very high overall speedups and this
should be the case on cluster as well as on GPU workstation.

The label before the ":" identifies locations in the code where SProf::Add was called
and the 16 digit numbers are microseconds since epoch timestamps. The other numbers are vm and rss memory in kb. 
Opticks has bash and python scripts that find and parse multiple of these SProf.txt 
from different runs and presents results, such as::
