### Postdoctoral Position in GPU-Accelerated Optical Photon Simulation for Neutrino Experiments

**Institution:** Institute of High Energy Physics (IHEP), Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing, China

**Field of Research:** Experimental Particle Physics / Neutrino Physics / Computational Physics

**Position Summary:**  

The Neutrino Physics group at IHEP is inviting applications for a postdoctoral
research associate to contribute to the development of Opticks, an open-source
software package that leverages NVIDIA OptiX for GPU-accelerated ray tracing in
optical photon simulations, seamlessly integrated with the Geant4 framework.
This position will focus on advancing Opticks capabilities and applying it to
the Jiangmen Underground Neutrino Observatory (JUNO) experiment, a
next-generation neutrino detector aimed at determining the neutrino mass
hierarchy and measuring oscillation parameters with high precision. The role
offers an exciting opportunity to work at the intersection of high-performance
computing, particle physics simulation, and large-scale neutrino experiments
within an international collaboration.

**Responsibilities:**  

- Collaborate on the enhancement and optimization of Opticks, including
  improvements to GPU-based ray tracing algorithms, integration with Geant4, and
  performance benchmarking.  

- Develop and implement Opticks within the JUNO simulation framework to
  accelerate optical photon propagation simulations, addressing challenges such
  as high CPU/memory demands in muon and neutrino event modeling.  

- Conduct detailed simulations and analyses for JUNO, validating results
  against experimental data and contributing to physics interpretations.  

- Participate in code maintenance, documentation, and open-source contributions
  to ensure Opticks remains a robust tool for the broader high-energy physics
  community.  

- Present findings at international conferences and publish in peer-reviewed
  journals.  

- Engage in collaborative efforts with JUNO team members and external partners,
  including potential travel to collaboration meetings.

**Qualifications:**  

- A PhD (or equivalent) in Physics, Computer Science, Engineering, or a closely
  related field, completed or expected by the start date.  

- Strong programming skills in C++ and Python; experience with GPU programming
  (e.g., CUDA) is highly desirable.  

- Familiarity with simulation tools such as Geant4, or experience in ray
  tracing, optical physics, or high-performance computing.  

- Prior involvement in particle physics experiments, neutrino detectors, or
  computational modeling is advantageous.  

- Excellent problem-solving abilities, attention to detail, and the capacity to
  work independently while thriving in a team environment.  

- Proficiency in English; knowledge of Chinese is a plus but not required.


**Conditions of Employment:**  

- This is a full-time position with an initial term of 2 years, renewable for
  up to 1 additional year based on performance and funding availability.  

- Competitive salary commensurate with experience, including benefits such as
  health insurance, housing allowance, and relocation support.  

- Access to state-of-the-art computing resources, including GPU clusters.  

- Start date: As soon as possible, ideally by early 2026.
