That release fixes several issues:

* muon crashes from non-optical particles crossing sensitive detectors that 
  caused corruption of Geant4 UserTrackInformation from point of view of 
  DepositEnergyAnaMgr or other AnaMgr

* incorrect qe_scale on GPU that caused more SPMT hits with Opticks than with JUNOSW


This release also has the prior fixes:

* WP_PMT hits should now be collected with collection efficiency culling applied

* collection efficiency culling of CD_LPMT hits applied, so hit counts 
  should closely match between Opticks and JUNOSW



Regarding manual settings::

    export CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0  ## still needed 

    export OPTICKS_MAX_BOUNCE=63   ## controls truncation of photon simulation
    export OPTICKS_MAX_TIME=1000   ##? (ns) also controls truncation 

       ## MAX_TIME may be preferable to MAX_BOUNCE, it needs testing 
       ## to find appropiate value that balances matching JUNOSW
       ## and not causing reduced performance to simulating 
       ## uninteresting long time tail photons

    ##export OPTICKS_EVENT_MODE=Minimal   ## Minimal is Opticks default, so not needed 
    export OPTICKS_HIT_MASK=EC            ## Still needed, next releases will not need this
